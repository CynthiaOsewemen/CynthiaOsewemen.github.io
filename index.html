Let's Talk

Data Analyst · AI & Automation · Financial Advisor · Author · Content Creator

11+ years turning operations, data, and financial planning into clear, actionable decisions.

I'm Cynthia Chidinma Osewemen, a data analyst, financial advisor, published author, and content creator based in Calgary, Alberta. Over an 11 plus year career I have led or supervised more than 30 staff, including the ground-up launch of a new bank branch, and delivered 20 plus data analytics and automation projects. Today I work as a Data Analytics Consultant at Amdari and supervise the Data Analytics function at DaintyMindz Laboratory, applying AI-driven automation to streamline reporting and workflows. Alongside that, I run a financial advisory practice, GrowWithCynthia, write practical guides on the French pathway to Canadian PR, and document my relocation and personal growth journey on YouTube. Bilingual in English and French.

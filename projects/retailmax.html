Objective

RetailMax needed a centralized way to understand revenue and profitability, customer value and loyalty, discount effectiveness, category, channel, and store performance, and the financial exposure tied to returned and cancelled orders. The project also surfaced KPI and data quality issues that needed validation before executive use.

What I Did

  • Designed a five-page connected dashboard architecture: Executive Overview, Customer Intelligence and Profitability, Pricing and Margin Intelligence, Product and Channel Performance, and Revenue Risk and Opportunities
  • Built executive KPI cards, customer segmentation and loyalty analysis, discount band analysis, product and channel breakdowns, and Top 10 store rankings
  • Configured interactive slicers and navigation across Year, Region, Category, Sales Channel, Order Status, Customer Segment, Loyalty Status, and Age Group
  • Built an explicit QA layer that flags measures requiring reconciliation rather than silently changing source values, and prepared the executive presentation and report

Achievements

Deep discounting of 31%+ generated only £2.93M in profit against £5.94M in discount value, a clear margin protection opportunity, while £68.22M in revenue (66.5%) tied to returned and cancelled orders was flagged as at risk and requiring validation. Beyond the dashboards themselves, the project demonstrates analytical maturity by documenting KPI reconciliation issues rather than presenting unverified figures as fact.

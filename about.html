Let's Talk

About

Eleven plus years across banking, AI-driven analytics, financial advisory, and building a life in Canada

The full story behind the dashboards, the advisory practice, and the YouTube channel.

I'm Cynthia Chidinma Osewemen, a data analyst, financial advisor, author, and content creator with over 11 years of experience across banking operations, AI-driven analytics, and workflow automation. Across my career I have led or supervised more than 30 staff, delivered 20 plus data analytics and automation projects, and built a reputation for turning messy operational data into decisions people can actually act on. I'm skilled in SQL, Power BI, Excel, Python, Tableau, and data visualization, with hands-on experience designing dashboards, defining performance metrics, validating data quality, and delivering actionable insights to stakeholders. I'm also a continuous learner who adapts easily to new tools, systems, and environments, drawing on my experience and skills to make better decisions, improve processes, and pick up new ways of working quickly.

My career started in banking in Nigeria, where I spent close to a decade moving from Customer Service Officer through Funds Transfer Officer, Service Officer, Branch Service Manager, and eventually Head of Operations at Keystone Bank and TAJBank. One of the achievements I'm proudest of is leading the operational start-up of a brand new TAJBank branch: I built cash handling procedures and service protocols from the ground up, aligned every process to bank policy, and onboarded and trained over 15 staff to get the branch running smoothly from day one. That launch, together with a customer service initiative that lifted satisfaction ratings by 85 percent and reconciliation improvements that cut transaction errors by up to 80 percent, is what convinced me that operations and analytics are really the same skill: reading a system clearly enough to make it better.

After relocating to Canada, I moved fully into data analytics and AI-driven automation. I currently work as a Data Analytics Consultant at Amdari, where I design, build, and maintain interactive Power BI dashboards, KPI reports, and analytical products that support business priorities and operational decision making. Alongside that, I supervise the Data Analytics function at DaintyMindz Laboratory, an AI research and education lab, where I lead analytics projects, mentor interns, and apply AI-driven automation tools to streamline data processing, reporting, and operational workflows for a professional network of nearly 4,000 followers on LinkedIn. I've also worked as a freelance Project Manager, Data Analyst, and CRM Admin on Upwork since 2022, a Top Rated freelancer, automating workflows across Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Jira, Airtable, Make.com, N8N, and many other tools that improved delivery speed by 25 percent and efficiency by 30 percent. I hold an MBA in Human Resource Management from Ahmadu Bello University, and I completed NPower Canada's Junior Data Analyst Program, which sharpened my technical skills and helped me understand how to navigate the Canadian workplace as I built my career here.

Through my Microsoft Azure AI-900 certification, I built a solid foundation in cloud computing, learning core Azure concepts, cloud service models, AI and machine learning workloads, computer vision, natural language processing, and responsible AI principles. It's an area I keep building on: as cloud and AI tools evolve, I continue learning how to apply them to real data and automation problems rather than treating the certification as a finish line.

Outside of my analytics career, I run a financial advisory practice, GrowWithCynthia, helping families in the US and Canada with financial education, solutions, and protection, including life insurance, critical illness and disability protection, and long term savings goals like the TFSA, FHSA, RRSP, and RESP, because I believe the same clarity I bring to a dashboard belongs in a financial plan. I'm also the author of practical guides on learning French for Canadian immigration, and I document my own relocation to Canada journey, including passing the TCF Canada and earning Express Entry through the French pathway, on my YouTube channel, Alijaz Diary, where I share lessons on personal growth, mindset, and adapting to life as a newcomer. I'm bilingual in English and French.
